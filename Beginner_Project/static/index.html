How to play

  1. Fly the day. Arrow keys or W A S D. Up and down climb. Left slows you, right speeds you up.
  2. Catch and dodge. Fish feed the chicks. Branches and crows hurt the nest. A gust shoves you.
  3. Last 8 days. Each flight is 20 seconds. Keep nest health and food above zero. The chicks eat every day.
