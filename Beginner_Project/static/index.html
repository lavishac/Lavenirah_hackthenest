How to play

  1. Read the day. The story tells you what is happening by the river.
  2. Pick a place. Fly the eagle there, or press the button under the story. Keys: 1 nest, 2 boat, 3 river.
  3. Last 8 days. Keep nest health and food above zero. The chicks eat every day.
